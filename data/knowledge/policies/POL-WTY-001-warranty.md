## 1. Purpose

Define the warranty terms, coverage, and claim procedures for all IndustraCool equipment sold to enterprise customers.

## 2. Standard Warranty Terms

| Product Line | Warranty Duration | Start Date |
|-------------|-------------------|------------|
| PX-100 Industrial Chiller | 24 months | Date of installation |
| PX-150 Precision Cooler | 24 months | Date of installation |
| PY-200 Commercial HVAC | 36 months | Date of installation |
| Accessories & Sensors | 12 months | Date of purchase |

**Fact:** warranty.px100_months = 24 months

## 3. Coverage

### 3.1 Included
- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
- Component failure under normal operating conditions (within rated specifications).
- Labor costs for on-site warranty repairs performed by certified IndustraCool technicians.
- Replacement parts (shipped at no cost to the customer).
- Emergency repairs for CRITICAL severity issues (SOP-042, SOP-071).

### 3.2 Excluded
- Damage resulting from improper installation (not performed by certified installer).
- Operation outside rated environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, altitude).
- Unauthorized modifications or third-party repairs.
- Consumable items: air filters, coolant, lubricants.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality.
- Acts of God, natural disasters, power surges (unless surge protector was installed per manual).

## 4. Warranty Claim Procedure

1. **Report**: Customer contacts support with equipment model, serial number, and issue description.
2. **Verify**: Support agent verifies warranty status in the Asset Management System.
3. **Diagnose**: Remote or on-site diagnostics per SOP-018.
4. **Authorize**: If covered, the system auto-authorizes the repair. No cost estimate needed.
5. **Execute**: Dispatch technician and/or ship parts per SOP-055.
6. **Document**: Record all warranty work in the service history.

## 5. Extended Warranty Options

Customers may purchase extended warranty coverage:
- **Silver**: +12 months, parts and labor.
- **Gold**: +24 months, parts, labor, and priority dispatch.
- **Platinum**: +36 months, all-inclusive with annual preventive maintenance.

## 6. Warranty Transfer

Warranty is transferable to a new owner if the equipment is sold, provided IndustraCool is notified within 30 days of the ownership change.
