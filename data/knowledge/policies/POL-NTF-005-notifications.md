# POL-NTF-005: Customer Notification Standards

**Document ID:** POL-NTF-005
**Version:** 1
**Category:** Corporate Policy
**Effective Date:** 2024-04-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-03-20
**Owner:** Customer Communications
**Approved By:** VP of Customer Success

---

## 1. Purpose

Define the standards and requirements for all customer-facing notifications sent by automated systems and support agents, ensuring consistency, timeliness, and professionalism.

## 2. Notification Types and Timing

| Event | Notification Method | Timing |
|-------|-------------------|--------|
| Ticket created | Email + SMS | Within 5 minutes |
| Technician dispatched | Email + SMS | Immediately upon dispatch |
| Technician en route | SMS | When technician departs |
| Technician arrived | SMS | Upon check-in |
| Issue resolved | Email | Within 30 minutes of resolution |
| Ticket closed | Email | Upon closure |
| Pending approval | Email to approver | Immediately |
| Approval granted/denied | Email to requester + customer | Within 5 minutes of decision |

## 3. Content Requirements

All notifications must include:
- **Ticket reference number** for tracking.
- **Clear, jargon-free description** of the current status.
- **Next expected action** and timeline.
- **Contact information** for follow-up questions.
- **IndustraCool branding** per brand guidelines.

## 4. Escalation Notifications

When an issue is escalated:
- Notify the customer that their case has been elevated for priority handling.
- Provide the name and contact of the escalation owner.
- Do NOT disclose internal escalation reasons or policy details.

## 5. Prohibited Content

Notifications must NOT contain:
- Internal system error messages or stack traces.
- Employee personal phone numbers.
- Financial details (costs, refund amounts) unless explicitly authorized.
- Speculative timelines or promises not backed by SLA commitments.

## 6. Channel Preferences

Respect customer channel preferences stored in the CRM:
- If a customer has opted out of SMS, use email only.
- If a customer prefers phone calls for CRITICAL issues, initiate a call.
