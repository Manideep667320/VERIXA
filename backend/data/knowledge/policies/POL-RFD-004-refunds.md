# POL-RFD-004: Refund and Credit Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RFD-004
**Version:** 1
**Category:** Corporate Policy
**Effective Date:** 2024-02-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-04-15
**Owner:** Finance
**Approved By:** CFO

---

## 1. Purpose

Define the conditions, limits, and approval requirements for issuing refunds, service credits, and goodwill adjustments to IndustraCool customers.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

### 2.1 Full Refund
- Equipment delivered with manufacturing defect and reported within 30 days.
- Equipment that fails to meet published specifications after 3 repair attempts.
- Order cancellation before shipment.

### 2.2 Partial Refund / Service Credit
- SLA breach (see POL-SLA-003, Section 5).
- Extended downtime caused by IndustraCool's delayed response.
- Parts backordered beyond published lead times.

### 2.3 Not Eligible
- Customer-caused damage or misuse.
- Change of mind after installation.
- Consumable items (filters, coolant) once opened.

## 3. Authorization Thresholds

| Refund/Credit Amount | Approver |
|---------------------|----------|
| ≤ $500 | Support Agent (automated) |
| $501 – $2,000 | Support Manager |
| $2,001 – $10,000 | Regional Director |
| > $10,000 | VP Finance |

## 4. Processing

- Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days.
- Service credits are applied to the customer's next invoice.
- All refunds/credits must be recorded in the financial audit trail.

## 5. Goodwill Adjustments

For customer retention purposes, support agents may issue goodwill credits up to $200 per incident without manager approval, limited to 2 per customer per year.
